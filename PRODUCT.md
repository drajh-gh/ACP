# ACP product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

ACP's technical specification selects TypeScript, PostgreSQL, DBOS, Codex workers
and a Next.js production console. The first isolated interaction prototype is a
standalone HTML/CSS/JavaScript artifact, not a replacement production framework.

## Users

David is the initial operator; SZS / SloSki is the initial project. He needs to
manage several concurrent requests without reconstructing their context across
email, trackers, coding tasks and deployment systems.

## Product Purpose

Turn project signals into prepared decisions, then follow approved work through
verification, outstanding obligations and communication to an approved final
outcome. The proposed attention-reduction target requires a measured live pilot;
neither a prototype nor local tests establish it.

## Positioning

One durable request can coordinate multiple ACP missions and keep its destination,
decisions and remaining obligations across worker and conversation lifetimes.
ACP is not a second project tracker or a second workflow runtime.

## Operating Context

Initial intake is manual submission and a configured Gmail label. Existing linked
tracker/GitHub events may follow a request. Broader intake comes later. Production
changes initially use reviewed manual handoffs and independent verification.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Backend execution records remain authoritative. The interface cannot infer effect
success, readiness or completed checks from a click. Outcome/scope approval and
exact effect approval are different. Client sends require exact recipients/content
review. Scope changes create revisions and invalidate affected approvals.

The current prototype has synthetic, in-memory data only. It must not connect to
ACP, providers, credentials or a live model. Final-outcome taxonomy and automatic
recurrence/closure policy remain review decisions before those durable behaviors.
Private request identity and mission links may proceed independently without
activating those policies or treating the prototype as accepted.

## Brand Commitments

The name is ACP until David selects a replacement. The confirmed airport operating
model organizes requests, journeys and checkpoints; actions retain literal labels.
Do not invent airport codes, passenger nationalities, schedules or live progress.

Product-interview records are source context, not production acceptance evidence.
Use authored sample cases in the prototype, never real mailbox contents or people.

## Product Principles

- Preserve the underlying request when a proposed solution is rejected.
- Separate checkpoint, final outcome, approval, execution and verification.
- Keep uncertainty and remaining obligations visible.
- Let the operator review a decision without reading raw logs.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic controls, reduced motion and narrow
screens must retain the decision, targets, environment and verification limits.
Status cannot rely on color alone. Preserve review context during updates.
